My Jobs

My Bookings

See the meetings booked with you through a booking page.

Overview

Go to My Jobs → My Bookings. The list is for booking hosts and participants. A user who can view only their own bookings still sees this item and the meeting activities tied to those bookings.

Administrators create the public pages under Settings → General → Booking Pages.

What you see

  • The booker's name and email.
  • Start and end, in the booker's timezone.
  • Status, and Meeting URL. An online meeting shows Join. A booking without a link shows a dash.
  • Answers to the page's custom questions.
