My Settings

Holiday

See the holidays that apply to you.

Overview

Go to My Settings → Holiday. The page title is My Holidays, and the line under it reads View the holidays that apply to you.

The list is the holidays assigned to the signed-in user. A holiday for every user is a Company Holiday. A holiday that names this user is a Personal Holiday. Holidays assigned only to other people do not appear.

The columns are Holiday Name, Start Date, End Date, Recurring and Holiday Type. Search filters the list. Creating and editing holidays stays under Company Information.
