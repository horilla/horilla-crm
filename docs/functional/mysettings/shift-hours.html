My Settings

Shift Hours

See the working hours assigned to you.

Overview

Go to My Settings → Shift Hours. The page title is My Shift Hours, and the line under it reads View the shift hours assigned to you.

The list is the shifts assigned to the signed-in user. The columns are Name, Shift Hours, Week Days and Time Zone. Shift Hours is Same Hour Every Day or Different Hour Per Day. Week Days shows the days and the hours, for example Monday - Friday (9 a.m. – 6 p.m.). Search filters the list.

These hours are what a booking page uses when it offers slots for that host.
